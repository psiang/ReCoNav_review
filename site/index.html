Task & Benchmark

From Route Following to Constrained Long-Horizon Navigation

Practical navigation instructions can additionally impose hard and soft constraints on target and route selection. We study constrained long-horizon VLN, where these constraints may apply to individual stages or the entire journey.

A benchmark for joint target and route satisfaction

To evaluate agents on constrained long-horizon VLN, we construct a benchmark of scene-grounded tasks. Tasks combine visits, their order, and scoped hard and soft constraints, measuring joint target and route satisfaction.

We construct a benchmark of 192 tasks across nine HM3DSem scenes, with RGB-D rendered in Habitat. For each task, we specify visits and constraints over scene entities, check that a feasible route exists, and express the task in natural language. Each task includes an exact start pose and annotations used only for evaluating the complete route. No tasks are used for training.

ReCoNav overview

RGB-D observations and the instruction are represented as an ASG and an instruction contract. The Determination Engine uses scene and task rules and route queries to derive and verify judgments. VLMs make remaining semantic judgments and use the engine’s evidence for repair. The engine returns a checked route.

Experiments

Full-instruction satisfaction (Full IS) requires a physically valid route satisfying required visits, orders, hard constraints, and all explicit preferences. We evaluate performance, examine global context and constraint reasoning, and ablate task modeling and the Determination Engine.
